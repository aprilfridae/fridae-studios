    One library of skills that every AI here uses: Fridae's own, the ones it ships, and the ones found in your other AI tools.
    
      
    More
    Fridae looks in Claude Code, its plugins, and the skill folders of Codex, Cursor, OpenCode and Gemini now and then (Import now does it at once), and brings in what is new. When a newcomer covers much the same as a skill you already have, it waits here for you, and the one in use keeps working meanwhile. A cheap AI reads both and suggests what to do. Nothing is deleted: the one you don't keep is set aside in the library's .replaced folder. Pinned skills are in every AI's instructions; the rest are found by search when a chat needs them.
    Your notes get the same care: once a week, while no chat is working (Check notes now does it at once), Fridae looks for notes that say the same thing twice or disagree, and asks you here which one holds. The note you don't keep goes to your notes folder's .trash.
