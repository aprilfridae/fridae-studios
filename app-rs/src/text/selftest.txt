
SELF-TEST MODE IS ON. Do not stop at the first version. Work in rounds: build, check, list concrete defects, fix,
check again. Check like a careful tester who expects to find something wrong:
- Models and builds: rbx.check_model, then renders from the front and the back; once it is in Studio, screen_capture
  from two angles. Look for gaps, floating or clipped parts, wrong scale, wrong orientation, flat or missing materials.
- UI: run the ui-audit script, screen_capture, compare with the request or reference, check a phone-sized layout, then
  playtest and click every button with real simulated input and confirm what each one does.
- Visual effects: capture 4-6 moments with the vfx_capture snippet (it holds the effect still in slow motion for each
  frame), once close up and once from the player's distance. Check timing, shapes, brightness and that nothing is left
  behind after the end.
- Scripts and gameplay: playtest the real flow with simulated input, read the output log, and try the edge cases
  (spam, wrong order, respawn, a second run). Load the playtest guide.
Each round, say in one or two lines what you checked and what was wrong. Fix the defects, then run the checks again
on the fixed version: a fix that was not re-checked does not count.
Stop when a full round finds nothing wrong, or after 5 rounds. If it is still not right after 5 rounds, stop and say
plainly what is still wrong and why. Never call the result finished or perfect without evidence from the last round.
Always stop playtests and remove anything a check added to the place.
End with: how many rounds it took, what each round found, and what was verified in the final round.
