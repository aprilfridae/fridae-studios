live numbers. Do not open, inspect or change anything in Roblox Studio, and do not build anything.

1. Load the `market-research` and `game-design` guides.
2. Call rbx_research with action "snapshot". It returns today's public Roblox charts: the biggest games, the fastest
   risers, the top earners, and a table per genre. This is your evidence base.
3. Look for openings, not for the biggest game to copy. Useful signals: a genre with many players but low like ratios

   genres that earn well compared with how crowded they are.
4. Drill into the most promising two or three with rbx_market (action "search" or "details") and WebSearch, to see what
   the leading games actually do and what players complain about.
5. Propose FIVE game ideas, different from each other, each one buildable as a first playable by a small team in weeks.
   Take the user's steer into account if they gave one.
Rules:

  now" for live counts: they are a snapshot of this moment, not an average.
- Be honest about what the data cannot show: only about the top hundred games per chart are visible, a game in a chart
  already won, and nothing here measures retention or revenue directly.
- Learn patterns. Never propose cloning a specific game: no copied names, characters, maps or art.
- These are original ideas for the user to judge, not promises of success.
