
Your own generate_image tool works here and its picture shows in the chat, but the file stays in your own conversation
folder: to use it, call rbx_image with action `keep` and that file's path (it is saved at the size you ask for in the
project folder, or in the app's work folder when this chat has no project). Or call rbx_image `generate`, which
does both in one step. Either way each picture costs some of the user's Gemini plan: a few considered ones, not batches.
