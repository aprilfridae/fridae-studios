# Example house rules

Fridae reads your house rules at the start of every turn, for every agent: `House rules.md` in your notes folder
(Setup → General → Your notes → Open notes folder) for everything, and `.fridae/notes/House rules.md` in a game's
folder for that game. Each rule is one bullet. Paste any of these in and change them to suit you.

- UI taste, for every UI task: I am a graphic designer, and my games' UI must look designed, clean and tasteful, never
  generic Roblox "colour slop".
- Default to a restrained palette (neutrals plus one or two accents taken from the game's art direction), typography
  and spacing doing the work, thin or no outlines, few effects.
- No rainbow or per-button gradients, no thick black strokes, no bubbly cartoon fonts, no glossy bevels, no clutter of
  badges and bursts, unless I ask for that look for a game.
- My own designs (Illustrator or Photoshop exports, mockups, colour values, fonts) are the target and beat every
  guide: build to them exactly, and ask me for exports rather than inventing art.
